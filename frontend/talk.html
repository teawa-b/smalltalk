SmallTalk Code

Know what to say next.

SmallTalk hears what they love, asks Qloo, and suggests what to say next. On glasses, a headset or your phone.

How it works

  1. It listens

    Six-second chunks of audio become text (ElevenLabs speech to text). Hold I'm talking or Space while you speak, so your lines aren't counted as theirs. Nothing is stored.

  2. Qloo builds their taste

    Every band, film, book, place or brand they mention is found in Qloo. Qloo says what people with that taste also love, and what you both share.

  3. You get a nudge

    Three short things you could say next, each tied to a Qloo result. On display glasses it shows one, large.
